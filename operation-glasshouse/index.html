OPERATION
GLASSHOUSE

Everyone has a story.
Make sure yours holds.

A social-stealth game in twelve floors. Walk in as nobody. Talk your way out as someone else. No script, no dialogue tree: they hear your voice, see your face, and decide whether to believe you.

Briefing & Rules

  1. The job. You are inside Meridian Tower. Get a keycard issued under a fake name and collect it from the floor 2 desk before 6:00 PM. You start at 9:00 AM.
  2. You talk, out loud. Walk up to a person and speak into the laptop mic. They answer through the speaker. No typing, no menus. What you say is the game.
  3. Conversations cost time. Every conversation burns 15 to 40 game minutes. Hesitation burns real seconds, and people notice.
  4. They listen. Pauses, filler words, over-politeness, and pressure raise a person's suspicion. Contradicting something you said earlier raises it more.
  5. They watch. You are standing in front of them, and your camera is your face. Looking away, a furrowed brow, a pressed lip, a badly timed smile: all tells. Camera off means they squint at you and start warier.
  6. They talk to each other. Between conversations, staff gossip. A story you told the receptionist may reach IT before you do. Use it or get caught by it.
  7. They can walk away from you. Push too hard and a person cuts you off mid-sentence. Push too far and your phone rings: Meera, the Security Lead. That is the only phone call in the game, and you get one chance.
  8. The map reveals itself. Every place you visit and every fact you extract lights up a room in the tower. Let people talk.
  9. Endings. Clean, Flagged, Burned, Clock-out, and one secret ending. Your Mission Report replays the exact moment you gave yourself away.
Tip: say who you are before they ask. Never change your story. Look at the camera like you'd look at a person.
